Fintech infrastructure · Türkiye

Automated trading and market-data infrastructure.

FFI builds and operates an in-house platform for multi-venue trading across centralized exchanges and on-chain markets — from real-time market data to execution, settlement and accounting.

Platform

Multi-venue execution

Cross-exchange arbitrage across CEX and on-chain venues with strict price-controlled orders, hedging and inventory rebalancing.

Market-data service

A shared real-time data layer that streams order books and prices from multiple venues to every service in the platform.

Signing & relay server

Central transaction signing and routing with per-application wallet isolation and transfer safety checks.

Ledger & analytics

Append-only accounting, invariant monitoring and dashboards that keep every trade and transfer reconciled.

Engineering with Claude

  • Multi-agent code audits covering correctness, money-path safety and security
  • Analysis of trade and ledger data, incident summaries and daily operational reports
  • Technical documentation and shared engineering standards in English and Turkish
